How it works

Watch. Enjoy. Give directly.

  1. 1

    Watch

    Slow, cinematic flights through the Star Citizen universe. No commentary, no hype, just the view.

  2. 2

    Pick a cause

    Browse vetted organizations that support children's education, filtered by your country or working internationally.

  3. 3

    Give directly

    You donate on the organization's own website. Nothing passes through us: no fees, no middleman, no "buy me a coffee".

Why trust this?

We never touch your money.

This project doesn't collect donations and doesn't earn from them. It only points you to organizations that do the actual work, and every one of them shows how it was checked: by a donation seal, a charity register or by us personally.
