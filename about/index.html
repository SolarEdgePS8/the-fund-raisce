About

Who's piloting here?

Calm Star Citizen flight films for a good cause. Enjoy the view and, if you wish, help children learn by supporting an organisation directly.

The original About page's full wording was not provided. This is a clearly marked reconstruction based on the original homepage.

Why this project exists

The Fund RaiSCe combines quiet cinematic flights through the Star Citizen universe with encouragement to support children's education. The videos are free to watch.

Where the donations go

Donations are not collected on this website. Any contribution happens directly on the selected organisation's own website.
