About

Who's piloting here?

Hi, I'm Sascha. I fly through Star Citizen, film the view, and hope it makes a few people give to kids' education.

The idea

You probably know channels that end every video with "buy me a coffee". This one ends with: if you enjoyed the flight, give that coffee money to an organization that helps children learn instead. Not to me.

The films are slow on purpose. Think aerial documentaries of the countryside, just set on alien worlds. Star Citizen 5.0 brought a new generation of planet technology, and the landscapes became too beautiful not to share.

Why I never touch your money

The project started as a different idea: charity races in Star Citizen. Unlike real motorsport, nobody can get hurt. I pitched it at Bar Citizen meetups in Stuttgart and Ulm and had great talks with fellow Citizens. But collecting and passing on donations properly needs a registered association, a lot of volunteers and a lot of paperwork.

So I flipped it around. I don't collect anything. I point you to organizations that do the real work, and you give to them directly. Every organization on the list shows how it was checked: a donation seal, an official charity register, or our own vetting for initiatives too small for a seal.

Why education?

Because curiosity is what got most of us into a game about exploring a universe in the first place. Every kid deserves the chance to learn, ask questions and think for themselves, wherever they grow up.
